Incentive Structure Reference

Incentives are counted ONLY for appointments with status Confirmed.

Bonus triggers at the 6th Confirmed appointment ($50). The 7th Confirmed appointment has no additional bonus. Tier bonuses then apply at 8th, 9th–12th, and 13th+.

Appointment Range Standard Bonus High Perf. (<25% Cancel)
